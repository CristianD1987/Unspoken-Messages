Thank you for reviewing Unspoken. Here is the information you requested.

A screen recording made on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached. Unspoken has no account registration and no login. It has no content shared between users, so reporting and blocking do not apply.
The only purchases are optional tips (Settings → "Leave a tip"): three consumable In-App Purchases (com.fofensoft.unspoken.tip.small / .medium / .large). Tips don't unlock any features or content. The whole app is free, and the recording shows a sandbox tip purchase.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
Unspoken is a private, offline space for expressive writing. Users write messages, in a familiar chat layout, to someone they can't or won't actually message: a person who has passed away, a former partner, an estranged family member, or a friend they lost touch with. Nothing is ever sent. Writing down things that were left unsaid helps people sort out their thoughts and move on. When they are done, they can mark the conversation as closed.
Audience: adults and teens (13+) who want a private place to write. Unspoken is a personal writing tool. It is not therapy, a medical device or a medical service, and it makes no health claims. The Terms of Use say so and point users in crisis to emergency services (112) and crisis helplines.

**3. How to use the app (no login, no credentials needed)**
1. Launch the app → read and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
2. Go through the short intro and onboarding → create a 4-digit PIN (Face ID can be enabled optionally).
3. On "Conversations", tap + → enter a name (optionally pick a contact or a photo) → open the conversation.
4. Type messages and send them. They appear as chat bubbles and are stored only on the device, encrypted.
5. From the conversation menu, "Closure" marks it as closed (read-only).
6. Settings → export a password-protected backup file (via the iOS share sheet) and import it back; change the PIN; withdraw consent / erase all data.
7. Background the app and reopen it → the PIN / Face ID lock screen appears.
8. Optional: Settings → "Leave a tip" → choose a tip. This is a standard In-App Purchase sheet, and nothing is unlocked.
No sample files are needed.

**4. External services**
None for core functionality. The app has no backend, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no AI services and no third-party SDKs that collect data. All content is encrypted and stored locally on the device. Fonts are bundled with the app. The only network communication is with the App Store, through StoreKit, for the optional tip In-App Purchases. The app has no external payment links.
iOS system features used only when the user chooses: Contacts picker (to fill in a name), Photo Library (to pick a picture), Face ID (to unlock), Files / share sheet (backup export and import).

**5. Regional differences**
None. The app works the same way in every region. It is in English only and needs no network connection.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
Not applicable. Unspoken does not provide medical, health, financial or other regulated services, and it does not contain protected third-party material.
