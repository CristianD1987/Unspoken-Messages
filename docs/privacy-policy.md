# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Unspoken is provided by FOFENSOFT S.R.L. (CUI 47274231), Voluntari, Ilfov, Romania. Contact: office@fofensoft.com.

## In short

- Unspoken does not collect, receive or share any of your data.
- There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- Everything stays on your device, encrypted.

## What stays on your device

The app stores only on your device:

- the names you give to conversations and any pictures you choose for them
- your messages
- your settings (such as biometric unlock and auto-delete)
- a protected (hashed) version of your PIN, never the PIN itself

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

## How it is protected

Your data is encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is created on your device and kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), set so it never leaves that device.

Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint unlock are handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app only receives a yes/no result and never has access to your biometric data.

When the app goes to the background, its content is hidden from the app switcher, and the app locks again after a short time.

## Permissions

- **Contacts** (optional): only used when you choose to pick a person from your contacts. The app reads the name of the contact you select. Your contact list is never uploaded or stored by us. You can use the app without granting this.
- **Photos** (optional): only used when you choose a picture for a conversation. The selected picture is resized and stored encrypted on your device.
- **Face ID / biometrics** (optional): used only to unlock the app.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Backups

You can export a backup from Settings. The backup file is encrypted with a password you choose, and you decide where to save or share it. We never receive your backups and cannot open them or recover your password.

## Links outside the app

If you choose to support the app, a Buy Me a Coffee page opens in your browser. That service is governed by its own privacy policy. Nothing from the app is shared with it.

## Information from app stores

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may provide us with anonymous crash reports and aggregate statistics (such as number of installs). These never include your messages, names or pictures.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual messages, all messages in a conversation, or whole conversations in the app. "Forgot PIN" erases everything. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your device. Optional auto-delete can remove old messages automatically.

## Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold your personal data, we have nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You are always in full control of your data on your device. If you have any privacy question, contact us at office@fofensoft.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP).

## Children

Unspoken is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date and show material changes in the app.
